The Recipe

CTFT — four ingredients.

C

Context

The situation, who's involved.

T

Task

What to do.

F

Format

Length and shape.

T

Tone

The voice.

A weak answer = a missing ingredient.

CTFT · 1 of 4 — Context

CContext — the setup.

It knows the world — not your situation.

✗ Weak
Write about our policy.
Whose? Which? For whom?
✓ Strong
I'm an HR officer at a Kuwaiti retail company. Staff may work from home two days a week from 1 November. It applies to all office staff.
Situation, audience, limits.
Context answersWho am I?What's the situation?Who is it for?Any limits?
CTFT · 2 of 4 — Task

TTask — the what.

Name the action.

✗ Weak
Help me with this.
Draft? Fix? Explain?
✓ Strong
Write a one-page memo announcing this policy.
One clear job.
Start with a strong verbWriteDraftSummariseAnalyseCompareRewriteListExplain

One main task per prompt.

CTFT · 3 of 4 — Format

FFormat — the shape.

Say nothing, and Copilot picks.

✗ Weak
Write a document.
What kind? How long?
✓ Strong
A memo: one short intro paragraph, then five bullet points. One page.
Now it knows the layout.
Be specific aboutLengthStructureDocument typeTable or bullets?
CTFT · 4 of 4 — Tone

TTone — the voice.

Same facts, different feel.

✗ Weak
Write an email.
To whom?
✓ Strong
Warm and encouraging — professional but friendly. It goes to all staff.
Pitched right.
Mix and matchFormalWarmConciseFirmFriendlyTechnicalPlain English

Hero Exercise · E1.7 From Zero To Hero · 15 min

One weak prompt. Four rounds. Watch it get visibly better.

A catering order arrived late. One ingredient per round. Rounds 2–4 on your own.

Round 0 · mushRound 4 · sendable
Round 0 · The Weak Prompt

Watch it be generic.

Run this — together
Write an email to a customer.
Output quality8%
Round 1 + Context

Give it the situation.

Add this
A customer who has ordered office catering from us every week for three years received today's order 45 minutes late. It was our scheduling mistake, and we have fixed the driver schedule.
Output quality32% — no task, so it guesses
Round 2 + Task

Say what to do.

Add this
Write them an email that apologises for the delay and reassures them it won't happen again.
Output quality55%
Round 3 + Format

Shape it.

Add this
Keep it under 120 words and give them a direct number to call.
Output quality76%
Round 4 + Tone & limits

Set the voice — and the limits.

Add this
Make it warm but professional. Don't promise a discount or refund. Sign off as the Customer Care Team.
Output quality95% — sendable
The Insight

Nobody nailed it in one shot — and that's the method.

Steer it: “shorter” · “warmer” · “in Arabic”.
