Block 3 · Excel

It's the 28th. Somewhere in your company, someone is about to spend four hours doing exactly what they did last month.

The real analysis is the last five minutes.

Think Of It Like…

An analyst pulling up a chair — who shows the working.

"What's driving the dip?"
→
a trend, a formula you can read, a chart
Excel — Three Moves

Analyse → Add → Visualise.

Analysefinds the trends
→
Addwrites formula columns
→
Visualisebuilds the charts

Formulas visible. If you can't see the working, you can't check it.

Before You Ask

Clean data in — better answers out.

✗ Messy
Merged cells · two header rows · blank rows · totals mixed into the data
Copilot misreads the table.
✓ Clean
One header row · one table per sheet · no blanks · Format as Table (Ctrl+T)
Copilot reads it cleanly.
Live Demo · Excel

Teams — Recap The Meeting

The meeting ended. Who does what, by when?

✔ Copilot can

  • Recap decisions, actions and owners from the transcript
  • Answer “What did I miss?” during the meeting
  • Draft the follow-up email

✕ It can't

  • Recap a meeting you had no access to
  • Change what people said
  • Send the recap to clients by itself
Needs: transcription or recording switched on. No recap button on your account? Upload the transcript to Copilot Chat — same prompts.
