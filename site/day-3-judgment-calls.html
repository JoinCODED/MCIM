Exercise E3.2 · Responsible AI

Judgment calls — would you let this through?

A colleague asks you a question. There is no answer key. These are real work situations where the right answer is not always clear. For each one, decide four things: what you can share with AI safely, what you must redact or refuse, whether to escalate, and the one line you would say to your colleague. Work with your table and discuss the difficult ones.

⚠ All fictional scenarios — no real customers, staff or figures.
