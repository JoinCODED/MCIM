Safe Use & Your Workflow

Use Copilot safely — security, compliance and Microsoft’s Responsible AI principles — then find prompts in the Prompt Gallery and build a workflow for your own job.

Thursday 1 October 2026 9:00 AM – 2:30 PM CODED, Kuwait

Day brief

The last day turns skill into habit. Block 1 covers Responsible AI: where your data goes when you prompt Copilot, why it only sees what you can see, what must never go into a prompt, and Microsoft’s six Responsible AI principles — then you practise with two exercises, Classify + Redact and Judgment Calls. Block 2 opens the Prompt Gallery (find, adapt, save and share prompts) and briefs the capstone: four role tracks, a light self-check rubric, and your own real task. After the post-workshop MAP test and lunch, you build your capstone: a workflow that chains two or more Copilot surfaces to solve a task from your job. No submission — it goes home with you. We close with certificates and a short survey.
